## Publication and evidence boundary

The historical predecessor acceptance covered owned startup/status/shutdown, eight correct normal/background responses and actual observation-to-estimate feedback. Its Windows owner is not shipped by the new portable gateway or optional source overlays. Earlier mixed-priority ordering and real Hermes parent/child checks are separate scenarios with their own source identities. Fresh gateway and source-reconstruction evidence is recorded separately in [evidence.md](evidence.md). These results do not qualify every public PR head, arbitrary protected tools, natural multi-generation compression, all native backends or performance gains.

In particular, Strata #1493 remains a scoped upstream draft. The standalone scheduler, runtime-owner packaging, #3399 composition and optional client additions must not be silently attributed to that PR. Hermes #135418 remains a generic post-approval seam; ordinary parent/child calls do not demonstrate model-selected protected-tool authorization. The second-Windows-PC requirement in #1324 is still unmet by work on the original machine.

Author and license credit is preserved through [research.md](research.md), the bibliography, source notices and pinned component provenance. PR discussion is evidence of what contributors reported, not an independent verification of every upstream claim.
